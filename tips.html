Stay Safe

While your complaint is under review, consider these steps.

📞 Contact Emergency Services

If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number.

👨‍👩‍👧 Tell Someone You Trust

Share your situation with a trusted friend, family member, or mentor.

📷 Preserve Evidence

Keep screenshots, emails, photos, CCTV information, or medical records if available.

⚖ Know Your Rights

Every victim deserves to be heard. You have the right to report harassment safely.
